1. Executive Summary

FieldTrack Pro is a mobile-first construction field management platform designed for small to mid-sized contractors, cleaning companies, security firms, and other field-based businesses. It enables company owners and supervisors to manage projects end-to-end: from setting up a job and assigning staff, to tracking their real-time location on-site, capturing geotagged photos with before-and-after evidence, logging daily site activity, and generating professional reports automatically.

The platform addresses the most persistent pain points in field operations: ghost visits, buddy-punch time fraud, undocumented change orders, missing safety records, and the hours lost to manual daily reporting. It combines GPS staff tracking, photo documentation, task management, materials logging, and automated reporting in one simple, affordable application accessible via mobile (iOS and Android) and web browser.

Target Users
General Contractors · Specialty Trade Contractors (Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC, Roofing) · Civil Engineering Firms · Commercial Cleaning Companies · Security Guard Firms · Property Management Companies · Pest Control Companies · Landscaping Businesses · Home Care Providers · NGO and Field Survey Teams

Platform Delivery
Mobile App (iOS + Android) for field workers and supervisors · Web Dashboard for company owners and project managers · Client read-only portal via secure web link · Offline-first architecture for remote and low-connectivity sites

2. Goals & Success Metrics

2.1 Business Goals
Eliminate time theft and ghost visits through GPS-verified, face-confirmed clock-in and clock-out.
Give company owners real-time visibility of every worker across all active job sites from a single dashboard.
Replace paper site books and WhatsApp-based task management with a structured, evidence-backed digital system.
Auto-generate daily site logs and weekly reports so supervisors spend less than five minutes on admin per day.
Provide photo-documented before-and-after evidence that protects contractors in client disputes and insurance claims.
Make construction-grade project management accessible and affordable for businesses with five to two hundred field workers.

3. User Roles & Permissions

The platform operates on a six-tier permission model. Each role sees only what is relevant to their function, reducing complexity for field workers while giving full control to company owners.

Role 1: Company Owner / Administrator — Full Access

Full, unrestricted access to all platform features across all projects and all staff. The primary account holder responsible for company setup and subscription management.

Capabilities
Create, archive, and delete projects, staff accounts, and client profiles.
View live GPS location of all staff across every active project on a single map.
Access all timesheets, payroll exports, financial reports, and project reports.
Set geofence boundaries for each project site.
Approve or reject change orders and timesheet submissions.
Configure notification rules, report delivery schedules, and billing settings.
Manage all user roles, reassign staff between projects, and deactivate accounts.
View complete audit trail of all activity across the company.

Role 2: Project Manager — Assigned Projects Only

Assigned to one or more projects. Manages project scope, team composition, scheduling, and reporting for their assigned projects only. Cannot access other project managers' data.

Capabilities
Create and manage tasks, phases, milestones, and punch lists within assigned projects.
View live location of workers assigned to their projects only.
Review and approve daily site logs and timesheets for their project team.
File and approve change orders, delay logs, and material delivery confirmations.
Generate and send progress reports and daily logs to clients.
Add or remove workers from their projects within the company staff pool.
Cannot access financial reports, billing, or workers on other projects.

Role 3: Site Supervisor / Foreman — On-Site Leadership

On-site leadership role. Manages the crew on a specific site, verifies work completion, and compiles the daily site log. Primary mobile app user among management-level staff.

Capabilities
View and assign tasks to workers under their supervision on the current site.
Verify task completion: review worker's photo evidence and mark as verified.
Complete the daily site log summary and submit for PM review.
File delay reports, safety incidents, near-miss reports, and change order requests.
Confirm material and equipment deliveries with photo evidence.
Log toolbox talks and safety briefings with attendee list and digital sign-off.
Cannot approve timesheets, access financials, or see workers on other sites.

Role 4: Field Worker — Mobile Only

The mobile-only role used by operatives on-site. The interface is intentionally simple: the worker sees only their tasks, their clock-in status, and the camera.

Capabilities
Clock in and out using GPS-verified, face-verified selfie at the job site.
View their personal task list for the day with details and priority.
Mark tasks in progress, upload before-and-after photos, and mark complete.
Add voice-to-text or typed notes to a task or a photo.
Log materials used on a specific task with quantity.
File a safety near-miss or raise a flag to their supervisor.
View their own timesheet and hours worked this week.
Cannot see other workers' data, financials, reports, or project settings.

Role 5: Subcontractor — Own Crew, Limited Scope

A separate company engaged for specific scopes within the main contractor's project. Has their own login and crew, but their data is visible to the main contractor.

Capabilities
Clock in their own crew members and track their attendance independently.
View and complete tasks assigned to their company by the main contractor.
Submit daily work log to the main contractor's project manager for review.
Upload compliance documents: insurance certificates, tax filings, and licences.
Track their own materials usage and submit delivery confirmations.
Cannot view the main contractor's financials, internal notes, or other subcontractors.

Role 6: Client — Read-Only via Secure Link

Clients receive a branded, read-only window into their project's progress. No account creation required — access via a secure, time-limited shareable link.

Capabilities
View project progress percentage and current active phase.
Browse the project photo gallery: geotagged, timestamped images selected by the contractor.
Read daily log highlights and weekly progress report summaries.
Review and digitally sign change orders submitted for their approval.
View key project milestones and their status.
Cannot see staff details, internal notes, financials, timesheets, or site GPS data.

4. Module 1 — Company & Staff Management

4.1 Company Profile Setup
Company name, logo, address, industry type, and primary contact details.
Default currency, time zone, and working-hours policy including break rules.
Company branding applied to all exported reports and client-facing documents.
Subscription plan management and billing control.

4.2 Staff Directory
A centralised, searchable record of every person in the company's workforce:
Full name, profile photo, phone number, email address, and emergency contact.
Trade and skill tag: Electrician, Plumber, Carpenter, Mason, Labourer, Surveyor, Driver, etc.
Employment type: Full-time Employee · Part-time · Day Worker or Casual · Subcontractor Staff.
Worker status: Available · Assigned to Project · On Leave · Inactive or Archived.
Certifications store: upload any certification document with expiry date. System sends 30-day expiry alerts automatically.
Worker document vault: employment contract, ID, proof of address, and tax documents.
Full GPS-linked work history: all clock-ins, projects worked, tasks completed, and hours logged by date range.

4.3 Subcontractor Company Profiles
Create a subcontractor company entry with company name, contact details, and trade type.
Add individual workers under the subcontractor company as a crew list.
Upload subcontractor compliance documents with expiry tracking: public liability insurance, employer liability, tax registration, and contracts.
Link the subcontractor company to one or more projects with a defined scope of work.
Subcontractor admin receives their own login and manages their crew independently within the platform.

4.4 Availability & Scheduling Overview
Company-level calendar showing every worker's current assignments colour-coded by project.
Double-booking detection: alert fires if a worker is assigned to two jobs on the same shift.
Leave and absence management: workers or admins log planned leave; system blocks scheduling during that period.
Capacity view: see total available worker-days for the next two weeks across the company.

5. Module 2 — Project Management

5.1 Project Setup
Project name, reference number, description, and project type (residential, commercial, civil, maintenance, and others).
Client name and contact details linked from the client directory or entered fresh.
Site address with map-pin placement and geofence boundary configuration.
Contracted start date, planned end date, and actual end date.
Project budget: total contract value and labour cost estimate for job costing comparison.
Multiple phases or sections within one project, each with its own start date and milestone.
Multi-site support: a single project can encompass multiple site locations.
Project status lifecycle: Tendering → Awarded → Active → On Hold → Completed → Archived.

5.2 Project Team Assignment
Assign a Project Manager as the primary responsible person for the project.
Assign one or more Site Supervisors or Foremen to the project.
Add individual field workers from the company staff directory with role and shift.
Invite subcontractor companies and specify their scope within the project.
Set each person's working days, shift hours, and applicable cost rate per project.
Template reuse: copy the team structure from a previous similar project and adjust as needed.
Workers receive a push notification when assigned, including site address and start date.

5.3 Document & Drawing Management
Upload and version-control project documents: blueprints, architectural drawings, permits, specifications, and contracts.
Documents accessible offline on mobile for field reference without internet.
Attach documents to specific tasks or phases (e.g., attach the electrical schematic to all electrical tasks).
Permit tracking: log permit type, issue date, expiry date, and issuing authority.

5.4 Project Timeline — Gantt View
Visual horizontal timeline showing all project phases and their tasks.
Drag-and-drop adjustment of phase and task durations on the timeline.
Task dependency links: Task B cannot start until Task A is marked complete.
Milestone markers at key project events such as foundations signed off, roof completed, and client handover.
Planned vs. actual progress overlay showing how far behind or ahead the project is running.
Delay propagation: when a phase slips, all dependent phases shift automatically with a warning notification.

6. Module 3 — Task & Work Assignment

6.1 Task Creation
Task name, description, and the project and phase it belongs to.
Assigned worker or workers: one task can be assigned to multiple workers simultaneously.
Priority level: Low · Normal · High · Urgent.
Start date, due date, and estimated hours to complete.
Attach reference photos, drawings, or instruction notes to the task before assignment.
Recurring task configuration: daily, weekly, or custom interval.
Task templates: save a task as a reusable template for future projects.

6.2 Task Status Lifecycle
Status	Who Sets It	Evidence Required
Not Started	System on creation	None
In Progress	Field Worker	Optional: progress note
Completed	Field Worker	Required: minimum one after-photo and a note
Verified	Supervisor or Foreman	Supervisor reviews photo evidence and confirms
Rejected	Supervisor or Foreman	Reason sent back to worker with correction notes
Overdue	System (automatic)	Alert sent to supervisor and owner

6.3 Before & After Photo Documentation
This is one of the most critical features in the platform. Every task includes a structured, enforced photo capture workflow:
Before photos: worker photographs the site condition before beginning work. Photos are automatically geotagged and timestamped.
Progress photos: optional mid-task capture for complex or long-duration tasks.
After photos: the platform enforces a minimum of one after-photo before the Complete button becomes active. Workers cannot bypass this requirement.
Side-by-side before and after view: supervisors and owners review paired photos in a split-screen comparison on both mobile and web.
Photo annotation: draw arrows, add text labels, or circle areas of concern directly on the photo before submitting.
All photos stored with full metadata: GPS coordinates, timestamp, task name, project name, worker name, and device ID.
Tamper detection: the platform flags any photo whose GPS metadata does not match the expected site coordinates.

6.4 Punch List / Snagging
Create a punch list linked to a project or a specific area within the project.
Each punch-list item includes: defect photo, description, location reference, responsible trade, and priority level.
Status per item: Open · Assigned · In Progress · Fixed · Verified.
Assign specific items to the responsible subcontractor; they see only their own items.
Track resolution time per item to identify slow-moving defects.
Export the full punch list as a branded PDF for client handover documentation.

7. Module 4 — GPS Tracking & Field Presence

7.1 Geofenced Clock-In and Clock-Out
Each project site has a configurable geofence boundary radius set by the owner, from 50 metres to 2 kilometres.
Workers can only clock in when physically inside the geofence of their assigned project; the clock-in button is disabled otherwise.
Clock-in requires a live face-verified selfie compared against the worker's registered profile photo. A mismatch triggers an immediate alert to the owner.
Clock-out is GPS-verified. Attempting to clock out from outside the site boundary raises a flag for supervisor review.
Offline clock-in: when connectivity is unavailable, clock-in data is stored locally and synced automatically when connection is restored.

7.2 Live Location Dashboard (Owner and PM View)
Interactive map showing the live GPS position of every clocked-in worker, updated every 60 seconds.
Filter by project: view all workers on a specific site, or all workers company-wide across all active sites.
Worker pins display: name, trade, current task, time since clock-in, and project name.
Out-of-zone alert: workers who move beyond the site boundary during work hours are flagged with a red pin and a push notification to the supervisor.
Historical breadcrumb trail: replay any worker's movement path for any past work day.

7.3 Time-on-Site Calculation
System automatically calculates total time on-site per worker per shift, per project, and per week.
Break deductions applied automatically per company-configured rules.
Overtime flagging: hours beyond the contracted daily limit are highlighted in the timesheet view.

7.4 Worker Location Privacy
Location tracking is active only during clocked-in hours. The platform does not track workers outside of work hours.
Workers are shown a plain-language disclosure at first login explaining what is tracked, when, and why.
Workers can view their own location history at any time from the app.

8. Module 5 — Daily Site Log & Field Notes

The daily site log is the most operationally significant output of the platform. It is auto-assembled from the day's captured data, requiring the supervisor to add only a brief summary.

8.1 Auto-Generated Log Content
Attendance: every worker on-site with GPS-confirmed clock-in and clock-out times.
Weather: automatically fetched for the site's GPS coordinates. Includes temperature, wind, precipitation, and active weather alerts.
Tasks completed today with completion photos and worker names.
Tasks in progress with current worker and estimated remaining time.
Incidents and near-misses filed during the day.
Materials received and any delivery discrepancies flagged.
Change orders raised or approved today.
Visitors logged on-site.

8.2 Foreman Summary Input
Single text field for work-performed summary. Voice-to-text supported for hands-free entry.
Issues encountered: open text field for anything not captured automatically.
Delay log: reason category selection (Weather, Materials, Client Access, Design Change, Equipment Failure, Labour Shortage, Other) with duration in hours.
Estimated progress percentage for the current phase: slider from 0 to 100 percent.
One-tap submit: log locked, PDF generated, and sent to configured recipients automatically.

8.3 Photo Notes and Field Annotations
Workers and supervisors can add free-form photo notes at any time, independent of a specific task.
Each photo note is geotagged, timestamped, and linked to the relevant project and date.
Photo notes appear in the daily log automatically and in the project photo gallery.

9. Module 6 — Timesheets & Payroll Preparation

9.1 Auto-Generated Timesheets
GPS clock-in and clock-out data is automatically converted into a timesheet entry per worker per day.
Each entry shows: project, date, clock-in time, clock-out time, break deduction, total hours, and cost code.
Workers can flag a timesheet entry they believe is incorrect, raising a query to their supervisor.

9.2 Cost Code Tagging
Each hour block is tagged to a project phase or cost code (Foundation, Framing, Electrical, Finishing, etc.).
Foreman assigns cost codes in bulk at end of day for the whole crew.
Individual workers can tag their own hours by task if greater granularity is required.

9.3 Approval Workflow
Supervisor reviews and approves timesheets daily from GPS-populated data.
Project Manager makes final approval before timesheets are released for payroll export.
Disputed entries are flagged and held from export until resolved.

9.4 Payroll Export & Integration
Export approved timesheets to Excel or CSV at any time.
Direct integration with QuickBooks Online and Xero for payroll processing.
Per diem and expense entries captured per shift can be included in exports.
Overtime clearly separated from standard hours in all export formats.

10. Module 7 — Materials & Deliveries

10.1 Material Usage Log
Workers log materials consumed per task: item name, quantity, unit, and notes.
Running total of materials used by type across the full project.
Comparison against the planned material allowance per phase if entered during project setup.
Over-consumption alert: PM notified when usage exceeds the allocated quantity.

10.2 Delivery Confirmation
Supervisor or worker confirms a delivery by photographing the delivery note and logging: supplier, items, quantities, and condition.
Short delivery flag: received quantity does not match ordered quantity — PM notified automatically with an action item created.
Damaged goods: photo and note flagged to PM and owner immediately.
Delivery log included in the daily site log automatically.

11. Module 8 — Safety & Incident Management

11.1 Incident Reporting
Any worker or supervisor can file an incident report in under 60 seconds from the mobile app.
Incident types: Near-Miss · Minor Injury · Serious Injury · Equipment Damage · Environmental · Property Damage · Security.
Required fields: incident description, GPS location (auto-captured), photos, persons involved, and immediate actions taken.
Severity classification: Low, Medium, High, Critical. Critical incidents trigger immediate SMS and push alerts to the company owner.
Corrective action assigned to a named person with a deadline and tracked to resolution.
Incident automatically included in the daily site log and safety report.

11.2 Toolbox Talks & Safety Briefings
Foreman logs a toolbox talk with: date, topic, attendees selected from the crew list, and notes.
Workers acknowledge attendance via a digital sign-off on their own phone.
Full toolbox talk history stored per project and exportable for safety audits.

11.3 Safety Checklists
Custom checklists built by the owner or PM (daily site opening check, weekly equipment inspection, scaffold inspection, and others).
Assigned to supervisor on a recurring schedule with mandatory completion before the start of the working day.
Checklist completion requires photo evidence for any flagged or failed items.
Failed items generate a corrective action automatically assigned to the relevant person.

12. Module 9 — Change Orders

Supervisors or project managers raise change order requests from the field with description, reason, estimated additional cost, and supporting photos.
Change order is tagged with GPS coordinates and timestamp of when it was raised.
Owner or PM receives an immediate push notification and reviews in the app.
Approval states: Pending · Approved · Rejected · Deferred.
Approved change orders increment the project's approved contract value automatically.
Platform generates a branded PDF change order document for client digital sign-off.
Full change order log per project with status, cost impact, and approval history.

17. Assumptions & Dependencies

Field workers have access to a smartphone (Android 8+ or iOS 14+) during work hours.
The company owner or designated admin is responsible for initial platform setup: adding staff, creating projects, and setting geofences.
Each worker must have their profile photo uploaded at registration to enable face-verify clock-in.
Internet connectivity is available at least once per day for data sync. Offline mode handles temporary outages.
Payroll integration requires the customer to hold an active QuickBooks or Xero subscription.
Weather auto-logging requires the site GPS pin to be correctly placed during project setup.

18. Glossary

Term	Definition
Geofence	A virtual boundary around a physical site. The system uses GPS to determine when a worker is inside or outside this boundary.
Face Verify	A biometric check comparing a live selfie taken at clock-in to the worker's stored profile photo, preventing buddy-punching.
Buddy Punch	The fraudulent practice of one worker clocking in on behalf of a colleague who is not physically present on site.
Daily Site Log	An auto-generated document compiled each evening recording the day's attendance, tasks, weather, materials, incidents, and foreman summary.
Before & After Photos	A structured pair of photos: one taken before a task begins and one on completion, providing enforced photographic evidence of work quality.
Punch List / Snagging	A list of defects or incomplete items identified during or after construction, each assigned to the responsible trade for resolution.
Cost Code	A reference code categorising labour hours to specific project phases or work types for job costing and budget tracking.
Change Order	A formal document recording and approving a change to the agreed scope, timeline, or cost of a project.
Geofenced Clock-In	A clock-in system that only allows a worker to record attendance when their device GPS places them within the defined site boundary.
Offline Mode	The platform's ability to record clock-ins, tasks, photos, and notes without internet connectivity, syncing automatically when connection is restored.
